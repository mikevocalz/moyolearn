# Accessibility review — the body layer under reduced motion

SOT-KEYWORDS: audit motion a11y reduced motion vestibular body layer captions

**Reduced motion is a render mode** (doc 22 §7, `reduced-motion.ts`). Verified for the new layer:

- `presence/humano.ts` takes `reducedMotion`; the delta into the idle engine is 0, so every body channel — weight shift, torso turn, shoulders, wrists, all ten fingers, gaze-away, head-follow — holds at rest. The stance (a pose, not motion) stays. Test: `humano.test.ts` "reduced motion writes no mouth and no arm lift".
- Mouth and blink are never scaled: the A2F face is speech-driven and is registered under `mouthScale` (never scaled) in `ANIMATED_SURFACES`; blink stays at the engine's rate. `assertMotionPolicyComplete` passes with the six new surfaces registered (`weight-shift`, `torso-turn`, `shoulder-wrist`, `finger-noise`, `gaze-away`, `a2f-face`).
- The PRNG stream is identical between modes (the engine still runs; the writer pins), so a reduced-motion golden of the head is the same head.
- No vestibular triggers added: nothing translates the camera; the largest new translation is 22 mm at the hip over ≥ 1.2 s; the largest rotation is a 4° torso turn over 0.8 s.
- The reveal dissolve (320 ms opacity) is not motion in the vestibular sense; under `useReducedMotion` the screen already resolves the tutor to `audio-only`, where neither surface renders (`tutor-screen.tsx`).
- Captions are unaffected: they are drawn by the stage above the thread, outside the `Freeze`, and nothing in this pass touches them.
- The loading line is real text (`Text variant="caption"`), read by a screen reader; the 3D canvas stays `aria-hidden` until it is the surface on stage.

Open: the per-band touch target on the presence rail is unchanged (doc 23 §4.4); nothing new is tappable.
