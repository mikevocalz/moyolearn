# The loading line (ux-copy pass)

SOT-KEYWORDS: audit motion loader copy band voice natalie getting ready

One line under the 2D mark while the 3D body is preparing/warming/pending. A name and a verb; never "Loading assets…", never a percentage on a child's surface; the CDN download progress (when the demo build takes that path) is a bar, not a number.

| Band | Line | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K–2 (`young`) | Natalie's getting ready… | present tense, her name, something a six-year-old does themselves |
| 3–5 (`child`) | Natalie's getting ready… | same |
| 6–8 (`teen`) | Natalie's on her way | shorter; no ellipsis-cute |
| 9–12 (`adult`) | Natalie's on her way | a teen hears "getting ready" as a toy |

Rejected: "Loading Natalie" (she is not a file), "Warming up" (jargon), "One moment" (apologetic — nothing is wrong), any spinner.

Implemented in `packages/app/features/tutor/tutor-avatar.tsx` (`loaderCopyFor`).
