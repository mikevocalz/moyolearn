## Existing owners and hard boundaries

Extend the existing CRM and schedule; do not build another calendar or copy their records into a new business database.
- [ADR 110](../decisions/adr-110-sessions-object.md) records that booking persistence, attendance capture and revenue rollups remain separate, unimplemented slices. `scheduled | completed | canceled | missed` are existing session status values, not evidence of an attendance workflow. AI `tutorSessions` is learner content, never a tuition or payroll input.
- [Enrollments](../../packages/payload/src/collections/Enrollments.ts) remains the institutional roster. A commercial tuition agreement must have a separate, explicitly designed identity and policy; neither an institutional enrollment nor CRM `Enrolled` proves a charge is due.
- [Better Auth billing](../../packages/auth/src/server.ts), [plan configuration](../../packages/auth/src/billing-plans.ts) and [subscription reads](../../packages/auth/src/subscription-reader.ts) own Moyo SaaS subscriptions and entitlements. A family's SaaS subscription and an organization's Moyo plan are distinct from tutoring tuition charged by that organization and compensation owed to its tutors. Keep their references, permissions and reconciliation separate.
- Preserve [Payload configuration](../../packages/payload/src/payload.config.ts), the Payload 4 baseline, PostgreSQL/Supabase and auth-schema separation, and isolated admin-vite. Use [the existing jobs package](../../packages/jobs/index.ts) for asynchronous work rather than introducing a competing queue. Infrastructure reuse does not prove an invoice/payment provider integration already exists.

## Implementation sequence and required facts

Each numbered slice needs its own complete, tested implementation. Later financial slices must not infer missing earlier facts or quietly enable live actions.

1. **Commercial agreement.** Define an org-scoped agreement with canonical family and authorized payer references, service/participants, effective period, currency, versioned price and cancellation/makeup policies, and explicit activation/termination states. Use immutable policy snapshots on booked/earned obligations so editing tomorrow's policy cannot reprice yesterday's work. Existing household contacts are not proof of payer authority or guardianship.
2. **Durable appointment.** Reuse the human Sessions object and existing schedule, with verified household/agreement links, actual tutor/availability reads and an atomic conflict-safe create/move path. Session-to-family/agreement fields are not present in today's schema and require an explicit additive design. Do not bill fixture events or infer a household from display names.
3. **Attendance fact.** An authorized actor records a scheduled session's completed/canceled/missed outcome, occurred/recorded times and allowed business reason. Do not add a silent `no-show` status alias. Corrections must preserve who changed what and why, with a concurrency precondition and an explicit reversal of downstream effects. Learner performance, transcripts and safety incidents are never attendance evidence for financial automation.
4. **Earned charges and makeup credits.** A deterministic policy evaluation turns an attendance/cancellation fact into charge/credit decisions, referencing the session and exact policy version. A credit has an immutable grant, optional expiry and audited consume/reinstate entries; concurrent use can consume it at most once. Cancellation and correction must not silently destroy value or create a second charge.
5. **Invoice and collections.** Snapshot priced line items, tax/discount inputs and currency before issuance. Draft, issued, partially paid, paid, void and credited concepts need an explicit supported state machine in the implementation. A local invoice being issued is not a successful provider payment. Reconcile provider-confirmed outcomes into payments/allocations; only permitted transitions change the outstanding balance.
6. **Tutor payable.** Compute earned compensation from authorized attendance and the effective tutor rate/policy, independently of the family's payment state unless a reviewed compensation policy explicitly couples them. A payable is an obligation, not a completed transfer. Corrections produce auditable adjustment entries. Scheduling staff must not acquire payroll approval or payment execution privileges merely by recording attendance.
7. **Guardian and reporting projections.** An authorized adult sees only their linked family's relevant invoices, payments, credits and receipts; never an org-wide financial feed. Staff reports derive from reconciled records and apply existing suppression rules. [The current revenue series](../../packages/app/features/ops/ops.data.ts) remains a fixture until an actual rollup replaces it. No price, paywall or upgrade prompt appears on a learner surface.

## Money, consistency and reconciliation invariants

- Persist amounts as integer minor units with an explicit supported currency and currency-specific exponent; never binary floats, parsed UI currency strings or an assumption that every currency has two decimal places. For the current USD lead field, `valueCents` remains cents. Validate precision/range and currency compatibility before totaling or allocating.
- Distinguish sales pipeline value, invoiced amount, paid/allocated amount, refunds, open balance and tutor payable. They are different measures; do not label a CRM estimate as collected revenue or sum unlike currencies. Document rounding, tax, discount and allocation order before implementation.
- Derive actor/tenant from `protectedOperation()` context. Services enforce state transitions and purpose-specific permissions; only server-only repositories access Payload. Recheck every family, agreement, session, invoice and payable reference under that tenant. Authenticated status alone is insufficient.
- Use stable org-scoped operation/idempotency keys and unique event/provider references. Duplicate attendance submissions, retries and duplicate webhook delivery must produce one financial effect. A repeated key with different semantic input must fail. Use concurrency preconditions for corrections and state changes; do not replay an old result into a newer record.
- Persist the business transition and its queued work/audit intent atomically, or use an explicit transactional outbox with a recoverable dispatcher in the existing jobs system. Provider calls cannot be part of a database atomic transaction; use idempotent requests and reconciliation to recover the gap between local commit and provider response.
- Verify provider event authenticity before accepting it; scope the provider account/object back to the correct org and obligation. Handle duplicate and out-of-order events, timeouts, partial payments, refunds, disputes and missing callbacks. A browser return URL or optimistic toast is not payment confirmation. Ambiguous outcomes remain pending/reconciling, with an actionable operator record.
- Corrections use traceable reversal/adjustment entries linked to their originals. Do not erase an issued financial fact by overwriting a total. Define privacy-safe retention/redaction separately from immutable financial history; do not copy learner content or credentials into an audit trail.
- No background worker may bypass the same scope, policy, deduplication and consent checks as an interactive operation. Record attempts and terminal failures without secrets; expose dead-letter/reconciliation work instead of reporting false success.

## Decisions required before live financial work

This contract intentionally does not select a merchant of record, connected-account/payment model, supported countries/currencies, tax treatment, tutor employment classification or statutory retention period. Resolve and record those product/legal/provider decisions before implementing the dependent live flow. Also specify who may approve refunds, waive fees, issue makeup credit, approve payroll and send reminders; these permissions are not implied by existing CRM membership.

Payment authorization, recurring collection terms, cancellation/refund policy, consent to financial communications and provider account ownership must be explicit. Use provider-hosted/tokenized payment collection; never store card data or credentials in Payload business records. No migrations, provider credential changes, live charges, transfers or deployment occur as part of this documentation PR.

## Acceptance scenarios for later implementation

1. Inquiry → linked trial → activated commercial agreement → durable appointment → attendance → invoice → confirmed payment/allocation → tutor payable retains canonical IDs and policy versions. The same family name in another org cannot become linked.
2. Concurrent overlapping appointments are rejected; two consumers racing for one makeup credit cannot both succeed. Retry after an uncertain response makes one appointment, charge, invoice or payable effect.
3. Canceled/missed/completed sessions apply the chosen policy exactly. Changing a policy or rate affects the documented effective period only; correcting attendance after invoicing creates an auditable adjustment rather than altering issued history silently.
4. Partial payment, refund, disputed payment, duplicate/out-of-order provider events, queue retry and a crash between commit and dispatch reconcile to the correct balance without duplicate collection or tutor payment.
5. Missing membership, wrong tenant, wrong family, unauthorized refund/payroll actor, stale update and consent-denied reminder fail closed. SaaS subscription changes neither settle tuition nor mark compensation paid. Lapsed-plan read/export and learner access follow existing entitlements.
6. Loading, empty, forbidden, pending, stale, failed, reconciling and confirmed states are distinct. No success is shown before the authoritative result. Cancel/Close, repeated submit and reopening on another device preserve correct state.
7. Reused RoleShell/semantic `@acme/ui`/tokens remain consistent; no dead navigation, fabricated financial metrics or marketing based on learner struggle. Adaptive UI follows current repository instructions and the [pinned tailored foldable skill](https://github.com/mikevocalz/universal-spatial-starter/blob/256a97b132803fd2fb8049f7a120cef9cb641f41/.agents/skills/mikevocalz-foldable-layouts/SKILL.md), with appropriate React/Expo/design skills, accessible actions and real device evidence where claimed.
