# Design handoff: D04 sign-in and plan

SOT-KEYWORDS: desktop handoff d04 sign-in session plan copy states accessibility

## Who and when

Guardians reviewing a child's work at a desk, tutors and teachers between
sessions, organization and district staff doing admin. Learners do not sign in
on desktop in this release; their accounts are signed out with an explanation.

## Sign-in pane (Workspace section, signed out)

Heading "Sign in to Moyo Learn" (or "Your session ended") · optional notice ·
Email (NSTextField) · Password (NSSecureTextField) · Sign In (default button,
disabled until the email has "@" and the password is non-empty) · Password Help…
(borderless, Mail to info@moyolearn.com). Return in the password field submits.
The password is cleared after every submit; the email stays.

| Notice | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| invalid-credentials | That email and password don’t match an account. |
| verify-email | Confirm your email first. We sent a link to your inbox; open it, then sign in here. |
| session-ended | You were signed out. Sign in again to continue. |
| learner-on-desktop | Learner accounts use Moyo Learn on the web or on a phone for now. |
| offline | Moyo Learn can’t reach the internet. Check your connection, then try again. |
| error | Sign-in didn’t work: <server message> |

## Signed in

Workspace shows the account name and active workspace ("Ada Lovelace ·
Guardian"). Menus: Moyo Learn > Sign Out (after Settings…); Workspace >
"Signed In as <email>" (disabled), separator, one checkbox per role the
session holds.

## Settings > Plan

One line per subscription owner: "You: Family, trial ends in 12 days",
"<Org>: Studio, active", "…ended. Everything you set up stays readable and
exportable." No red, no countdown styling. Then "Plan changes and account
deletion happen on app.moyolearn.com. Your browser may ask you to sign in there
too." · Manage Plan in Browser… · Delete Account in Browser… (last).

## States

| Surface | Loading | Error | Empty | Offline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Session | "Checking your session…" | error notice | sign-in pane | offline notice; a stored cookie is kept on focus checks while offline |
| Plan | "Loading your plan…" | "Couldn’t load your plan: <reason>" | "No plan on this account." | same as error, with the offline message |

## Accessibility (not yet run live)

Notices use `accessibilityRole="alert"`; fields carry "Email" and "Password"
labels; the secure field uses `textContentType="password"` so Passwords
autofill can offer saved credentials. VoiceOver and Full Keyboard Access passes
are open items.
