# Competitive Feature & Pricing Matrix: Tutoring-Business CRM / Management Software (2025–2026)
### Prepared to inform the Moyo Operations Cloud CRM buildout (US market)

## 1. Executive Summary

**Bottom line:** The tutoring-ops software market is a fragmented mid-market where scheduling + billing are fully commoditized, but a true sales-grade CRM (customizable pipeline, activity timeline, source attribution, win/loss) is rare — only TutorCruncher and the newer entrants Tutorbase and Noto genuinely have one — and no incumbent combines a modern CRM, a real automation/workflow engine, AI-assisted scheduling, and an ethical enrollment health/at-risk score in one product. That combination is Moyo's whitespace. The most defensible wedge is a **best-in-class importer + modern UX + native automation engine**, because the loudest, most consistent user complaints across incumbents are dated interfaces, steep learning curves, weak/nonexistent lead pipelines, missing mobile apps, and unpredictable transaction-fee bills.

Three structural findings:

- **Pricing has bifurcated** into (a) flat per-month subscriptions (TutorBird ~$16.95, Teachworks $16.49–$187.99 base, Teach 'n Go $79+, Noto $49+), (b) percentage-of-invoice models (Tutorbase 1% when you invoice; TutorCruncher 0.5–1% on offline payments + 3.85%/3.5% card), and (c) marketplace commissions (TutorOcean 5–20%). Flat per-student pricing correlates with centers/schools; %-of-invoice correlates with newer, growth-stage sellers courting switchers.
- **The competitive-ethics flag is real and specific:** Tutorbase openly markets progress reports, test scores, and lesson performance as a "retention and upsell engine" with CRM "sales-team tags" including an explicit "At-risk retention" tag that auto-triggers sales follow-up. This is exactly the pattern Moyo's hard business/learning data wall forbids — useful as a differentiation-on-ethics story, not a feature to copy.
- **AI-assisted scheduling is a single-vendor differentiator today** (Tutorbase's "Find Slot"/"Find Spot"), and **enrollment health/at-risk scoring done ethically (business signals only — attendance, cadence, payment, engagement — never mastery data) is genuine whitespace.**

## 2. The Matrix — Platform by Platform

### TutorCruncher (CRM benchmark; agency/franchise)
- **CRM depth:** Strongest incumbent. Customizable client pipeline from first contact to booking, activity feed, business-level analytics (lesson hours, revenue, attendance, tutor performance), tutor-student matching, sales-category analytics, client spend over time. Client-manager assignment.
- **Scheduling:** Recurring lessons (online + in-person), availability, tutor "apply for opportunities" by skill/geography. Batch scheduling exists but reviewers call it time-consuming.
- **Billing & payments:** Automated e-invoicing, split payments/payroll. Per TutorCruncher's own US pricing page, "Card payments are charged at 3.85% and 3.5% for Pay as you Go and Startup customers respectively," plus a 1%/0.65%/0.5% offline-payment fee by tier. Stripe, GoCardless, Xero, QuickBooks Online integrations. US reviewers complain there is no client ACH option.
- **Payroll:** Comprehensive automatic tutor payouts, split payroll.
- **Comms:** Email + SMS (per-message), reminders. Built-in video calling ($0.01/min after free minutes; recording $0.03/min + storage).
- **Automations/API:** Powerful API on Enterprise; website plugins for signup/conversion. Reviewers note plugins/enquiry forms are limited without a developer.
- **Reporting:** Deep — revenue, tutor performance, retention, funnel.
- **Education-specific:** Custom lesson reports, tutor ratings.
- **Pricing:** Pay-as-you-go $30/mo, Startup $80/mo, Enterprise custom (Capterra); US docs list extra branches $50/mo, white-label $100/mo, priority support $120/mo. 2-week free trial. Card fee 3.85% (PAYG) / 3.5% (Startup). At scale the percentage layer is material — a school collecting ~$200,000/yr in US tuition pays roughly $7,700 in TutorCruncher percentage fees alone before Stripe's own costs (Teach 'n Go analysis, 2026).
- **Platform:** Mobile-accessible web; multi-branch; white-label domain. Integrations: Stripe, GoCardless, Xero, QuickBooks, Lessonspace, Outlook, Google Calendar, Mailchimp.
- **Market position:** Agencies/franchises/multi-tutor. G2/Capterra ~4.6. Weaknesses: steep learning curve (3+ months onboarding reported), unannounced updates/maintenance disruptions, limited customization without a developer, clunky navigation, no client ACH (US).

### Tutorbase (AI-scheduling bar; modern all-in-one; new entrant)
- **CRM depth:** Built-in visual pipeline + lead capture from website and WhatsApp; automated lead follow-ups. One of only three incumbents with a genuine CRM.
- **Scheduling:** Category-leading. Drag-and-drop calendar; recurring lessons auto-generate future instances; room management prevents double-booking; Google Calendar sync. **AI "Find Slot"** cuts new-student booking from 10+ min to under 2 min by generating valid teacher/room/time combos; **"Find Spot"** fills empty group seats.
- **Billing & payments:** Lessons auto-become invoice line items; hourly, packages, prepaid credits (wallet with low-balance alerts), custom pricing; cancellation fees + credit notes. Stripe, PayPal, Xero.
- **Payroll:** Per-completed-lesson auto payout; hourly/per-lesson/per-student/revenue-share; weekend/holiday premiums; one-click batch approval.
- **Comms:** WhatsApp integration (95% open-rate claim), automated reminders.
- **Automations/API:** Automated billing workflows, lead automation. Integrations: Google Calendar/Meet/Workspace, Lessonspace, Mailchimp, PayPal, Slack, Stripe, WhatsApp, Xero, Zapier, Zoom.
- **Reporting:** Attendance, payroll from calendar; progress tracking, lesson notes, progress reports.
- **Education-specific:** Lesson notes, learning paths/tracks, progress reports, assessment reports.
- **Pricing:** Free to use; **1% only when you invoice** through the platform. No monthly fee, no per-student fee. 21-day free trial, no contracts. Free migration support (3–5 days claimed).
- **Platform:** Cloud, mobile-friendly; multi-location; ~10–12 integrations.
- **Market position:** Solo tutors → centers (5–50 teachers) → language academies → franchises. Newer, less track record; "some advanced enterprise features still in development."
- **⚠ Ethics flag (verbatim intel):** Tutorbase's own blog ("Progress Report Template Tutoring," Dec 2025/Jan 2026) states progress reports are "a revenue tool that fuels retention, supports renewals, and unlocks referrals," calls the monthly progress report "your retention and upsell engine," and prescribes a feedback template stored in "Internal CRM with sales-team tags" carrying "Ready for upsell / Needs nurture / At-risk retention" tags that auto-generate "sales follow-up" tasks. It confirms score/assessment data feeds this pipeline ("that data should auto-populate your billing system and trigger a sales follow-up task"). **Direct violation of Moyo's business/learning data wall.**

### Noto (withnoto.com — direct ops competitor)
- **CRM depth:** Built-in CRM + lead management capturing inquiries and automating follow-ups; form builder syncs sign-ups to CRM; "sales command center" manages instructor availability/openings across locations; online-review generation.
- **Scheduling:** Private + group classes, conflict-preventing booking, multi-location.
- **Billing & payments:** Most-flexible-billing claim — deposits, class packs, automated recurring billing, and **direct ACH bank payments (~0.5%)** to cut card fees (positions savings up to ~$10k/mo for large centers vs card-only).
- **Payroll:** Built-in staff/payroll management referenced.
- **Comms:** Email, SMS, in-app messaging, newsletters, notifications; staff + parent portals.
- **Automations:** Pre-built lead-nurturing automation "out of the box"; marketing automation (email/SMS/in-app).
- **Reporting:** Financial metrics, room occupancy, staff retention analytics.
- **Education-specific:** Lesson notes, file sharing via portals.
- **Pricing:** Starting **$49/mo flat** (Capterra), free trial available.
- **Market position:** Lesson-based businesses (tutoring centers, music schools). Positions against Teachworks/TutorCruncher/TutorBird as the integrated modern option. Limitation: feature set may overwhelm solo/very-small operators.

### Teachworks (deep billing/QuickBooks/multi-location; dated UI)
- **CRM depth:** Core tutor/student management; reviewers/competitors note **no integrated CRM or lead pipeline** — needs external tools for lead management. Custom fields, teacher profiles.
- **Scheduling:** Color-coded calendar, six views, drag/drop/resize; recurring; group + private; website booking.
- **Billing & payments:** Rule-based pricing, branded invoices, bulk payment, Stripe; QuickBooks Online integration is a differentiator.
- **Payroll:** Teacher hours pulled from calendar → payroll calculation.
- **Comms:** Email templates, automated email/SMS reminders, Mailchimp.
- **Automations/API:** Developer API (Growth plan+ only), Zapier. Add-on library.
- **Reporting:** Attendance, hours, business performance, custom reports.
- **Education-specific:** Lesson notes, lesson tracking; weaker test-score tracking (user complaint).
- **Pricing:** Usage-based: Starter $16.49/mo + $0.32/student-lesson; Growth $47.99/mo + $0.189; Premium $187.99/mo + $0.065. API needs Growth+; multi-location branches need Premium (+$35/mo per branch beyond first 5). 21-day free trial (all features).
- **Platform:** Cross-device web, **no native mobile app** (complaint); multi-location; Stripe, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, Outlook, Mailchimp, Lessonspace, Excel, Zapier.
- **Market position:** Small education businesses (98% small companies; music/language/driving/test prep). G2/Capterra ~4.6. Weaknesses: dated interface, no mobile app, unpredictable per-lesson billing, timed feature downgrades, clunky test-score tracking, some features tier-gated.

### TutorBird (affordable solo/small; "not a CRM")
- **CRM depth:** Weak. Competitors describe basic statuses + bulk email; no lead pipeline (manage inquiries in spreadsheets). Student management, parent/student portals.
- **Scheduling:** Calendar, calendar sync, attendance tracking, recurring lessons, basic student portal booking.
- **Billing & payments:** Invoicing, online payments, reminders via Stripe, PayPal, ACH. Complaint: "invoices are actually statements," can't match payments to appointments; some want Zelle/direct transfer.
- **Payroll:** Payroll/paystubs for tutors, multi-tutor.
- **Comms:** Automated reminders; email. Complaint: no admin notifications when tutors book/cancel/reschedule.
- **Automations/API:** Limited; website integration (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress), Zoom.
- **Reporting:** Basic.
- **Education-specific:** Lesson notes, attendance, parent/student portals.
- **Pricing:** ~$16.95/mo Basic (one tutor); +$4.95/mo per extra tutor/admin; unlimited students/storage; 30-day free trial. (Some listings cite $14.95.)
- **Platform:** Web; **no dedicated app** (complaint); integrations Stripe, PayPal, Google Calendar, Zoom, website builders. Operated by Port 443, Inc. (Burlington, ON).
- **Market position:** Solo tutors/small centers/test-prep. Capterra ~4.8 (264 reviews). Weaknesses: no CRM, no app, QuickBooks integration missing, limited customization, statements-not-invoices.

### Teach 'n Go (parent portal + native mobile on every plan)
- **CRM depth:** Dedicated leads/prospect-to-student conversion pipeline (unlimited prospects). Better than TutorBird/Teachworks on lead capture.
- **Scheduling:** "Powerful scheduling," group classes, attendance, video-call integration, built-in booking.
- **Billing & payments:** Automatic billing, custom pricing/fees, combined payments, PayPal, invoicing, receipts.
- **Payroll:** Not a headline strength (positions on flat pricing/portal/mobile).
- **Comms:** Group email & SMS, student/parent portal.
- **Automations/API:** API access on all plans (per vendor comparison); some third-party listings claim no API (conflict — treat as **unverified**; vendor says API on all plans).

- **Pricing:** Flat **per active student**: from $79/mo (up to 100 students) US framing; €69/€89/€119/€219 tiers (EUR) up to 1,000+ students; scales to 2,000+. All features on every plan; 14-day free trial, 30-day money-back. **Native iOS + Android app on every plan** (key differentiator).
- **Platform:** Native mobile; 55+ countries. Multi-location supported.
- **Market position:** Language/music schools, tutoring academies, learning centers. Predictable flat pricing is its wedge vs Teachworks' variable model.

- **Market position:** Brick-and-mortar centers, agencies, contract/grant-funded programs. Weaknesses: hard to learn/train, clunky UI, database update quirks. G2 product direction rated below TutorCruncher.

## 3. Feature-Gap Analysis

**Table stakes (everyone has them — must be excellent, not differentiating):**
- Recurring lesson scheduling, availability management, calendar sync (Google/Outlook).
- Attendance tracking, lesson notes, parent/student portals.
- Invoicing + online card payments (Stripe near-universal).
- Email/SMS reminders and templates.
- Session-based tutor pay/payroll calculation from the calendar.
- Basic reporting (revenue, hours, attendance).
- Multi-location/branch support (mid-market and up).

**Differentiators (only 1–2 vendors do them well):**
- **True sales-grade CRM pipeline** (customizable stages, activity timeline, source attribution, win/loss): TutorCruncher, Tutorbase, Noto (and Practice/HoneyBook/Salesforce/HubSpot from outside the vertical). Most tutoring incumbents (TutorBird, Teachworks, My Music Staff) lack it.
- **AI-assisted scheduling** (auto slot/room/tutor generation): **Tutorbase alone** ("Find Slot"/"Find Spot"). Classcard's "Min AI" is adjacent but not scheduling-grade.
- **Native mobile app on every plan:** Teach 'n Go (native iOS/Android), Practice, TutorOcean, Setmore. Notably absent: Teachworks, TutorBird, My Music Staff (recurring complaint).
- **ACH-first low-fee billing:** Noto (~0.5%), Fons (~1.8%). Most card-only incumbents sit at 2.9–3.85%.
- **Contract/grant-based invoicing** (district/agency): Oases Online.
- **Out-of-the-box lead-nurturing automation:** Noto, Tutorbase. TutorCruncher requires developer work.
- **Prepaid credit/wallet with low-balance alerts:** Tutorbase, Wise (session credits), Noto (class packs).

**Whitespace (nobody does it well — Moyo's opening):**
1. **Ethical enrollment health / at-risk scoring using business signals only** (attendance trend, booking cadence, payment status, reschedule/cancel frequency, comms responsiveness) — explicitly **excluding mastery/learning data**. Today the only "at-risk" tagging in the vertical is Tutorbase's, which is built on the forbidden learning-data blend, and Salesforce's, which is institutional and grade-based. A business-signal-only health score is genuine, defensible whitespace.
2. **A modern automation/workflow builder** (visual trigger→action, plus Zapier/Make/webhooks/quality public API) native to a tutoring CRM. Incumbents either lack automations (TutorBird, My Music Staff), gate the API (Teachworks Growth+), or require a developer (TutorCruncher).
3. **First-class importer / low-switching-cost onboarding.** Only Tutorbase and Noto advertise free migration; incumbents' switching friction (and TutorCruncher's 3+ month onboarding) is the biggest unearned moat to attack.
4. **District/institution-grade reporting for SMB price** — funnel→revenue→utilization→outcomes dashboards a center could hand to a school-district contract officer, without Salesforce-tier cost. Oases (contract/grant) and Wise (district partnerships) gesture at it; nobody nails it affordably.
5. **A hard, marketed business/learning data wall** as a trust feature — the inverse of Tutorbase's upsell-engine positioning.

## 4. Pricing Analysis

**Where the market clusters:**
- **Solo/very small:** $13–$20/mo flat (TutorBird ~$16.95, Teachworks Starter $16.49, My Music Staff ~$13–$14.95, Acuity ~$16, Setmore free–$12/user).
- **Growing centers:** $49–$99/mo (Noto $49, Teach 'n Go $79, Classcard $99, Wise Essential $49).
- **Larger/multi-location:** $150–$220+/mo flat or usage (Teachworks Premium $187.99 base, Teach 'n Go Platinum €219, Fons ~$149.95, Classcard Growth $199, Wise Professional $349).
- **Enterprise/generic ceiling:** $75–$150+/user/mo (Salesforce Enterprise $87, HubSpot Pro $100 + onboarding, HubSpot/Salesforce Enterprise/Unlimited $145–$150).

**Which models correlate with which segments:**
- **%-of-invoice (Tutorbase 1%; TutorCruncher offline 0.5–1% + 3.5–3.85% card)** → growth-stage sellers courting switchers; aligns vendor upside with customer GMV; attractive to price-sensitive small sellers but produces unpredictable bills at scale — a documented driver, e.g. Teach 'n Go's breakdown notes "a tutoring agency processing $10,000 in monthly payments would pay $385 in card fees alone, on top of the $30 subscription… your monthly bill fluctuates with your revenue."
- **Flat per-student (Teach 'n Go, Noto)** → centers/schools that want budget predictability; the explicit counter-positioning against Teachworks' variable per-lesson model.
- **Per-lesson + base (Teachworks)** → low-volume operators; penalizes busy months (complaint driver).
- **Per-seat (HubSpot, Salesforce, Setmore/Acuity partly)** → enterprise/generic; expensive for tutoring's many-low-value-users shape.
- **Marketplace commission (TutorOcean 5–20%)** → demand-generation, not ops.

**What a combined learning-product + ops-suite can justify:** Moyo is not selling a $16 scheduling utility; it bundles a consumer learning product with a full ops suite (CRM/scheduling/billing/payroll/comms/reporting). That supports **premium flat-tier-plus-usage pricing anchored to the ops value ($79–$199/mo center tiers, in the Noto/Teach 'n Go/Classcard band), not to the learning app.** A defensible entry play is **free-to-start with a low %-of-invoice (undercutting TutorCruncher's 3.5–3.85% card and matching Tutorbase's 1% narrative) while charging flat for the CRM/automation layer** — capturing switchers on transparent pricing, then monetizing ops depth. Crucially, because of the data wall, Moyo **cannot** justify price via mastery-driven upsell mechanics the way Tutorbase does; the value story must be operational efficiency + ethical trust, not learning-data monetization.

## 5. Top User Complaints / Switching Levers

Synthesized from Capterra/G2/Trustpilot/Software Advice across incumbents:

1. **Dated/clunky interfaces & steep learning curves** — TutorCruncher (3+ month onboarding, "difficult to navigate," cluttered post-update dashboard), Teachworks (dated UI), Oases (hard to train staff).
2. **No real CRM / lead pipeline** — TutorBird ("manage inquiries in spreadsheets"), Teachworks (no lead pipeline), My Music Staff (no CRM).
3. **No native mobile app** — Teachworks, TutorBird, My Music Staff.
4. **Unpredictable/opaque billing** — TutorCruncher (%-based + card fees make monthly totals hard to predict; invoice line items unclear to accountants), Teachworks (per-lesson variability).
5. **High transaction/card fees; missing ACH** — TutorCruncher (3.85%/3.5% card, no US client ACH), general card-vs-ACH gap (Noto/Fons weaponize this).
6. **Weak/limited customization without a developer** — TutorCruncher (custom CSS/white-label ~$100/mo + dev), "had to write our own integrations."
7. **Missing/weak QuickBooks integration** — TutorBird, older TutorCruncher complaints.
8. **Invoices that are really statements / can't reconcile payments to sessions** — TutorBird.
9. **Unannounced updates & maintenance disruptions** — TutorCruncher.
10. **Poor online booking / self-serve friction & missing admin notifications** — Teachworks (booking setup), TutorBird (no alerts on tutor book/cancel/reschedule); self-service friction hurting trial conversion.

Each complaint is a switching lever an importer + modern UX + transparent pricing can win on.

## 6. The "What Moyo's CRM Must Beat" Checklist

1. **Beat TutorCruncher on CRM depth without its learning curve:** customizable pipeline (first-contact→booking), activity timeline, source attribution, win/loss — usable in days, not 3 months.
2. **Match Tutorbase's AI-assisted scheduling** (auto tutor/room/time slot generation, zero double-bookings) — while keeping scheduling suggestions free of any learning-mastery inputs.
3. **Beat everyone on the importer / switching cost:** one-click migration from TutorCruncher, Teachworks, TutorBird, My Music Staff; free migration support as standard (match/exceed Tutorbase & Noto).
4. **Ship a native mobile app on every plan** (beat Teachworks/TutorBird/My Music Staff; match Teach 'n Go).
5. **Offer ACH-first, low-fee billing** (beat 2.9–3.85% card-only incumbents; match Noto's ~0.5% narrative) with packages/prepaid credits/wallet + low-balance alerts and family credit balances.
6. **Native visual automation engine + quality public API/webhooks + Zapier/Make** on all paid tiers (beat Teachworks' gated API and TutorCruncher's developer-required customization).
7. **Ethical enrollment health/at-risk score built only on business signals** (attendance, cadence, payment, responsiveness) — the data-wall-safe answer to Tutorbase's mastery-based upsell tagging.
8. **Transparent, predictable pricing** (flat center tiers + optional low %-of-invoice) — beat TutorCruncher/Teachworks bill unpredictability.
9. **District/institution-grade reporting at SMB price** (funnel, revenue, utilization, tutor performance, attendance, exports) — approach Salesforce depth without Salesforce cost.
10. **Deep, native billing/accounting integrations** (QuickBooks + Xero) done right — fixing the #1 recurring integration complaint.
11. **Session-based payroll** with per-service rates, premiums, and one-click batch approval (match Tutorbase; beat the manual incumbents).
12. **Market the hard business/learning data wall as a trust differentiator** — explicitly no mastery-based sales triggers, in contrast to incumbents who blend them.

## 7. Recommendations (staged, with thresholds)

**Stage 1 — MVP wedge (0–6 months): win switchers on UX + import + transparency.**
- Build the importer first (TutorCruncher, Teachworks, TutorBird, My Music Staff CSV/API import) and a modern, learnable CRM pipeline + activity timeline. Ship a native mobile app.
- Price: free-to-start + 1% on-invoice, matching Tutorbase's narrative while flat-charging the CRM/automation layer at a $79–$99 center tier.
- *Threshold to proceed:* if trial-to-paid conversion clears ~15–20% and median migration completes in <5 business days, the wedge is working; if migration drags past 2 weeks, over-invest in import tooling before adding features.

**Stage 2 — differentiation (6–12 months): automation engine + AI scheduling + ethical health score.**
- Ship the visual automation builder (trigger→action), quality public API/webhooks, and AI-assisted slot-finding to reach feature parity with Tutorbase's scheduling.
- Launch the business-signal-only enrollment health score — the flagship ethical differentiator.
- *Threshold:* prioritize AI scheduling only once ≥30% of accounts run multi-tutor/room scheduling (below that, the ROI is thin and a simpler conflict-detector suffices).

**Stage 3 — up-market (12–24 months): district/institution reporting + ACH at scale + multi-location/white-label.**
- Add contract/grant-style invoicing (Oases pattern) and district-grade funnel/utilization/outcomes dashboards to court school-district contracts (the Wise/Paper GROW lane).
- *Threshold:* pursue district features once you have ≥3 multi-location or district pilots requesting them; otherwise stay SMB-focused.

**What would change the plan:** If a well-funded entrant (e.g., Tutorbase) ships an ethical, business-signal health score first, shift Moyo's differentiation to the data-wall trust story + district reporting. If card-fee compression makes ACH savings immaterial, drop ACH from the headline pitch and lead on automation + UX.

## 8. Caveats

- **Verified vs inferred:** Prices for TutorCruncher, Teachworks, TutorBird, Teach 'n Go, Tutorbase, Noto, Wise, Salesforce Education Cloud, and HubSpot are from current vendor/review sources and are reasonably firm (though tiers change frequently and annual-vs-monthly framing varies). **Unverified / quote-only:** Oases Online base price, Tutorware, TutorOcean's SaaS (non-marketplace) tier, myTutoring, Reg4Class. HoneyBook and Practice exact tiers vary by source and are marked approximate.
- **Vendor-blog bias:** Several data points (Teach 'n Go's competitor comparisons, Tutorbase's "vs" pages and progress-report blog, Noto's roundups) are competitor/self-serving marketing; feature and fee claims from these were treated as directional and cross-checked against Capterra/G2/GetApp where possible. Notably, the Tutorbase upsell-engine quotes come from Tutorbase's own marketing — which is exactly why they are strong evidence of intent.
- **US-only compliance framing:** Relevant regimes are FERPA (education records), COPPA (under-13; amended rule effective June 23, 2025, general compliance date April 22, 2026, now covering voiceprints/biometric identifiers and stricter data-retention limits), and 100+ state student-privacy laws (e.g., California SOPIPA, NY Education Law 2-d). No EU framing applied. Some vendors (e.g., Tutorbase) advertise "GDPR compliant"; for Moyo's US framing, prioritize COPPA/FERPA/state-law posture.
- **Data-wall design constraint (fixed):** Moyo keeps a hard wall between business data and learner learning data — no mastery-based sales triggers. This report flags where incumbents violate that (Tutorbase's explicit upsell/at-risk tagging built on progress/test-score data; Oases' softer CRM+assessment merge) as competitive-ethics intel, and recommends no pattern that crosses the wall.
- **Recency:** Current date August 21, 2026; pricing captured from 2025–2026 sources. Verify live pricing pages before any pricing decision.
