## Mobbin pass: learner-today-6-12

Archetype: **Invitation**. Eyebrow + H1, hero card with subject art left and Start right, an "Up next" strip, quiet due-work list below.

### Purpose

The screen an 11-18 year old lands on. Older learners will tolerate density that a 5-year-old will not, but they punish anything that reads as a chore list or a guilt ledger.

- **Where am I** — a named course or subject context, stated in text, not implied by artwork. This band reads.
- **What matters now** — the hero card. One session, its topic, its length, and a Start affordance in a fixed position.
- **What can I do** — Start the hero, glance at Up next, or open something from the due list. Three tiers, decreasing weight.
- **What changed after I acted** — the hero rotates to the next session and the completed item moves into the quiet list with a completion mark. No score, no bar filling toward a target.

### Take

- **Eyebrow carries provenance, not branding** (Mimo `DAILY REVIEW`, Brilliant `RECOMMENDED`). One uppercase word or two that tells the learner *why this item is here*. Moyo's set: `DUE TODAY`, `DAILY REVIEW`, `PICKED FOR YOU`, `FROM YOUR HOMEWORK`.
- **State the time cost next to the H1** (Mimo `10 min`). A teenager deciding whether to start needs the price before the pitch. This is the single highest-value line on the screen.
- **Start lives inside the hero card, on the trailing edge** (Babbel). Not a floating bottom bar, not a separate button below. The card is the unit; the action belongs to it.
- **Let the Up next strip get cut off at the right edge** (Babbel). Two half-visible cards teach horizontal scroll without a hint label or a pager.
- **Quiet list rows are art + plain-language "here you'll learn" + a verb** (Babbel learning plan). The verb changes with state (`Start` / `Repeat` / `Review`) while the row shape stays identical.
- **Progress is a sentence, not a meter** (Babbel `8/12 lessons completed`). Fits the no-pressure rule while still answering "how far in am I".
- **Outline the one row that is currently yours** (Uxcel Go). Border weight, not color fill, marks the active item.
- **Expand the hero into named sections before committing** (Speak). Warm-up / main / practice, one line each, then one Start.

### Refuse

- **Leaderboard tab in the bottom bar** — Mimo (https://mobbin.com/screens/54b61209-26b8-4d28-ac60-9f888e099b6e). A permanent destination built on ranking peers. Direct violation of the engagement-pressure ban.
- **"Your Practice Progress" stat tiles (`3 activities done`, `20m time on tasks`)** — Mimo (https://mobbin.com/screens/54b61209-26b8-4d28-ac60-9f888e099b6e). Time-on-task as a displayed metric turns study into a number to grow. Moyo shows what is left to do, not how long the child has been sitting there.
- **Streak flame + gem count + XP token header row** — Duolingo (https://mobbin.com/screens/c9474d08-3ce5-4dad-b3b1-76bc6649c518). Same rule as the K-2 doc.
- **Photographic face as the hero card's subject art** — Babbel (https://mobbin.com/screens/6a031570-7a5a-4007-a0f0-4ccf5dc47e1b). Structure adopted, imagery refused: Moyo hero art is a subject object or a cut-paper vignette, never a photographed person on a learner surface.
- **Purple-to-lavender gradient hero with a glossy 3D robot** — Mimo (https://mobbin.com/screens/54b61209-26b8-4d28-ac60-9f888e099b6e). No blur, no gradient. Moyo's hero is a flat paper field with an ink border.
- **Ghosted lock icons on future items** — Liven (https://mobbin.com/screens/1aa3b784-2b8d-4263-b412-0d342add307f), Uxcel Go (https://mobbin.com/screens/dba87d9e-9955-49be-be73-686477e601ee). Showing a padlocked future is a pressure device. Moyo simply does not render work that is not yet assigned.
- **`30 days / 30 lessons / 20 practices` commitment tiles** — Liven (https://mobbin.com/screens/1aa3b784-2b8d-4263-b412-0d342add307f). Front-loading total scope onto a learner's home screen creates dread, not orientation.
- **Emoji as row icons in a plan list** — Finch (https://mobbin.com/screens/c229ad54-ed26-4dec-80d1-399d54964f31). Emoji-as-art ban.

### Open questions for critique

1. Does the accent belong on the Up next strip, or does putting it there imply the *next* item matters more than the hero the learner is supposed to start now? Alternative: accent the eyebrow pill.
2. Mimo shows time cost; Babbel does not. Does a displayed `10 min` reduce avoidance, or does it become a negotiating chip ("only 10 minutes then I'm done")?
3. Babbel keeps completed items in the plan with a check. Should Moyo's quiet list keep the day's finished work visible, or collapse it behind a "done today" disclosure so the list only ever shrinks?
4. This band covers 11-18, which is two different people. Does one Invitation layout hold for both, or does 16-18 need the hero demoted to a row?
5. What renders when nothing is due and nothing is recommended? None of the references solve an honestly empty state — Babbel's loading skeleton (https://mobbin.com/screens/af715e9f-3b74-4de5-b014-55fa6748aa34) is the closest thing indexed, and it is a skeleton, not a design.
6. Where does the tutor-room entry point live on this screen? The hero's Start goes into a session; there is no established slot for "I have a question right now" that does not turn the home into a chat surface.
