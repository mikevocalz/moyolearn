# Mobbin pass: parents

Chapter 6 of the Moyo marketing site. Register: long-form **magazine / editorial** — serif measure around 65ch, a caption system, whitespace used as a luxury signal, duotone photographic treatment, and trust and safety explained in plain language.

**Structure only.** Where a row mentions serif or duotone, it is naming the *role* the treatment plays in the layout, not prescribing a typeface or a colour pair — Moyo's tokens decide those.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kinfolk | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1557474b-af5b-48e0-b8d3-541af616713b) | A full-width hairline rule opens the section, then a kicker in caps, a serif deck, and an issue reference set tiny beneath — the rule plus the shrinking type sizes do all the hierarchy work with no boxes, no cards and no colour. |
| Zellerfeld | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/09a9a70b-70f6-49d9-8966-d5524e46f120) | A two-column letter: the addressee sits alone in a wide left column and the entire body is set in a single narrow measure on the right, closed by a signature and a name-and-role line — the left column stays empty on purpose, and that emptiness is the formality. |
| Claude | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e8938edf-d08c-4334-ad11-e379609da9d7) | Metadata (category, date, reading time, share) is set as a small labelled list in the right margin beside the title rather than as a byline strip under it, and a rule separates the header block from the body — the article's "spec sheet" lives in the margin where it can be ignored. |
| Intercom | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/cd16a01d-3d63-47f2-a1eb-0ac0e5f9cbf7) | The standfirst is set noticeably larger than the body, and a series note sits in a tinted inset block with its own italic measure — one inset panel is enough to mark "this is context, not the argument". |
| SSENSE | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4de98a06-dbff-4e54-83c6-a301c519bba0) | A caps display headline with a serif deck directly beneath it and a single small outlined link, all centred with large air above and below — an article opener that is entirely type, and stronger for having no image. |
| The New Yorker | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/29df74de-a58b-43c5-aec5-3367dfb88e47) | A section label sits centred above a two-up of duotone-treated photograph on the left and a kicker/headline/deck/byline stack on the right, all centred within its own column — the tint unifies wildly different source photos into one editorial voice. |
| The New Yorker | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7b8ee3c6-6b9d-4058-a654-295734f0233a) | A full-bleed photograph carries an inset, centred caption stack — kicker, headline, one-sentence deck, byline — placed low in the frame where the image is quietest, so the photo is never cropped to make room for text. |
| Shop | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/581a9bd7-9e53-487a-a8ad-1f63b99768d1) | Four trust claims in a 2×2 separated only by hairline rules, each with a short plain-English headline, two sentences, and a link to the actual policy — no shields, no badges, and the link makes the claim checkable. |
| Grain | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/38457aa0-3ffd-44c6-8238-4b277e9128ba) | Three privacy principles as a plain checked list in one narrow measure, followed by a small paragraph giving the full policy URL and a real human email address — the escape hatch to a person is part of the trust statement. |
| Campsite | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7dd8f0d7-cc02-4852-8557-7eaf89de6fa7) | The security claim gets one inverted panel with a single icon and two lines and then the page moves on — refusing to pad safety into a six-cell grid is itself the confidence signal. |
| Sprout Social | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f57ccfe9-2901-4b6e-9744-dfccc623c821) | A short plain-language summary sits in a tinted band directly under the title, with the detailed technical prose beneath it in a smaller size — the two-tier read lets a parent stop after the summary without missing the point. |
| Canva | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/573de197-4e3c-43c0-a53c-11a7494d46b1) | A sub-navigation (Overview / Security / Privacy / Legal) sits above the trust section as its own tier — signalling that safety is a *place* with several pages, not a paragraph, before the reader has read a word. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer.io | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4d7ccf09-e699-46f8-b59c-a0b8ac745dec) | A grid of compliance badge tiles (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO) as the trust section | A badge wall. It communicates only to buyers who already know the acronyms and says nothing to a parent; worse, one tile reads "pending", which the badge format hides rather than explains. |
| Maze | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b07657a6-9166-45b2-bdc3-3fc8e29e4534) | Six security cards over a saturated patterned field with "Contact sales" as the section's only action | Six cells of acronym-led copy is a compliance checklist wearing a marketing costume, and putting a sales CTA inside the trust section makes safety feel like an upsell. |
| Grammarly | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/dea5069a-40ea-442b-8b34-ee2b9ad101e7) | Trust headline paired with a stock photo of a smiling handshake in an office | Stock handshake imagery is the visual equivalent of "we take your privacy seriously"; it depicts corporate reassurance rather than any actual practice, and no parent has ever been reassured by it. |
| Eventbrite | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ccc52090-36a7-4629-be76-4c711f2b4467) | Editorial index with an "Editors' picks" carousel plus a listicle sidebar | Listicle headlines and a rotating picks carousel are a content-farm register; the parents chapter has to read as a considered piece of writing, not a blog roll. |
| Pitch | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e2383164-f6dc-4917-86a5-36bd483d9d97) | Numbered "essential reading" row above a grid of purple gradient 3D-icon article thumbnails | The numerals are set so pale they are nearly invisible while the thumbnails shout — the hierarchy is inverted, and the gradient 3D icon set is an explicit hard-avoid. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "long form editorial article layout with serif body text in a narrow measure and generous whitespace"
- `search_sections`: "trust and safety explained in plain language with privacy commitments and how data is handled"

**MCP note:** this was the richest chapter of the pass — Kinfolk, The New Yorker and Zellerfeld are all genuine editorial specimens rather than SaaS pages imitating one. The gap is **duotone**: only The New Yorker returned an actual tinted photographic treatment, so the duotone system will need to be specified from the design language rather than referenced from Mobbin. Caption *systems* (numbered figures, credits) are also essentially unindexed here.
