# Mobbin pass: schools

Chapter 7 of the Moyo marketing site. Register: **Swiss / newspaper** — duotone data graphics, columned rigor, dense information architecture that reads "serious infrastructure", and an enterprise "talk to us" motion that is explicitly **not** a price grid.

**Structure only.** The point of this chapter is density done with discipline; take the column logic, the rule system and the ordering of the ask, never the visual identity.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Büro | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7bca883d-0960-4e65-917f-48e5880a4f46) | A year scale runs vertically down the far-left edge as a rail while the content beside it changes measure per block — a wide serif pull-quote, then a narrow justified column, then a second narrow column — so density and rhythm come from *varying* the measure against a fixed rail. |
| The Leap | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/08503cc8-10af-4837-af74-792e53d0c932) | A genuine bordered data table with three labelled columns whose header row is **repeated** partway down the table — the single most useful move in this whole pass for long institutional tables, and the reason it stays scannable past the fold. |
| Upwork | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/628dc701-15b6-4444-9517-591272c30e8f) | A left rail lists every sibling document with the current one marked, and the document itself opens with a numbered table of contents before any prose — the reader sees the shape of the whole corpus before entering one part of it. |
| Dub | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/825205b6-8aa3-4195-893d-0c348638a02e) | An "on this page" list is placed *inside* the content panel on the right rather than in the page chrome, with the active anchor marked by a vertical bar — the index travels with the document instead of belonging to the site. |
| Current | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2612c55d-585e-4378-bdb1-964a8aaff91f) | Title, a hairline rule, one plain-language sentence about what the document does, then a bulleted "Topics" list phrased as questions — the questions are the navigation, so a reader can find their concern without reading legal prose. |
| Mural | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/eeb94012-7dcf-4f29-9507-cea33728031f) | The left rail is grouped under headed sections (Terms, then Policies, then Security) and a "Print page" link sits above the document — treating the corpus as an organised binder, and acknowledging that institutional readers print things. |
| Aside | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c603fba7-9f62-4666-9109-26f84f912d90) | Kicker, one-line promise, one-line qualifier, the contact button, *then* the six-item capability list — the ask comes before the evidence, so a buyer who is already convinced never has to scroll past a list to act. |
| ElevenLabs | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b8117dc7-f766-4526-85df-309d2197c918) | The enterprise offer is one kicker, one three-line sentence and one button occupying the entire top half of the viewport with nothing else — refusing to list features at all is what makes it read as infrastructure rather than a plan tier. |
| Webflow | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d3c29e6c-e991-4772-98dd-9c84cfeb823a) | The enterprise block is one inverted panel: name and ask on the left, a 2×2 of *procurement* concerns (billing, security questionnaires, SLAs, scaling) on the right, and a customer logo row along the bottom inside the same panel — it answers the buyer's actual objections rather than listing product features. |
| Stripe | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ec71864f-b0dc-45c8-995b-47c7caa6af96) | The "talk to us" band is phrased as a question about the reader's situation, states what will happen after contact ("our teams will review your current statements"), and sits alongside a logo wall — describing the process removes the fear that "contact sales" means a trap. |
| Maze | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f3c5c566-f710-4ed7-a907-09520c8483bd) | A short custom-solution ask on the left and a 3×3 logo grid on the right share one row, and the full comparison table is pushed *below* it behind its own heading — the conversational route is offered before the grid, not after. |
| Hex | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/90b0e145-26c1-4efe-a6b1-b5141d2351c6) | Capabilities sit as a two-column checked list on the left and the ask is isolated inside a thin outlined box on the right — a hairline rectangle, with no fill and no shadow, is enough to make a CTA a destination in a dense layout. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teachable | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5b23eb17-d5db-4ef0-a3c3-57ca247a8654) | Enterprise tile sitting in the same row as the consumer plan cards, beside a "not sure which plan? take the quiz" tile | It reduces an institutional conversation to a fifth column in a price grid, and the quiz manufactures indecision in a reader who had already decided. Exactly the price-grid motion the brief rules out. |
| Miro | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/73347f02-cb6d-4675-9362-c21a96ac4f31) | Centred enterprise headline over a large product screenshot | Generic centred hero with a screenshot standing in for evidence — no density, no data, nothing an institutional buyer can evaluate, and the screenshot is unreadable at that size. |
| Customer.io | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/99c5b5d0-94d4-4e79-8ff3-6afadd19f8a3) | Legal document as an unbroken wall of body copy with a flat, ungrouped left rail | Adopted Upwork's and Mural's rails and refused this one: with no grouping and no table of contents, a fifteen-item flat list of policies is a filing cabinet with the labels removed. |
| ElevenLabs | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b8117dc7-f766-4526-85df-309d2197c918) | Abstract flowing-ribbon render occupying the lower half of the enterprise section | Took the type-and-single-button restraint, refused the ribbon: an abstract render is filler that says nothing about the service, and in a "serious infrastructure" register any decorative graphic reads as a hedge. |
| Maze | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f3c5c566-f710-4ed7-a907-09520c8483bd) | "Compare all plans" table opening directly beneath the enterprise ask | Adopted the row above it and refused this: dropping a feature matrix immediately under the institutional CTA pulls the reader straight back into consumer comparison behaviour. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "dense information rich section laid out in strict columns like a newspaper with rules and small type"
- `search_sections`: "enterprise contact sales section inviting institutions to talk to the team instead of showing prices"

**MCP note:** the density query resolved almost entirely to **legal and policy pages** — terms of service, privacy policies, data tables. That turned out to be a gift rather than a miss, since those are the only pages in the index with real information density, and the schools chapter needs exactly that register. Büro is the only true Swiss/editorial-grid specimen returned. **Duotone data graphics were not found at all** across either query; that half of the chapter has no reference here and must be specified from the design language.
