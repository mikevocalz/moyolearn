# Mobbin pass: bento

Chapter 2 of the Moyo marketing site. Target moves: asymmetric numbered-cell grids; bento that is **not** rounded-rect-icon-headline; mixed-media cells inside a strict grid; an oversized numeral as a cell; a cell whose content escapes its own boundary.

**Structure only.** Colour, fill, and type below are irrelevant — only the cell geometry, the ordering, and the escape moves transfer.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI in Design Report 2026 | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/bde9e82a-b11e-446f-9858-76c20256b468) | The chapter numeral and its one-word name form the entire left column of the cell, and the prose sits in a narrow right column — the number is the label, not an ornament stuck above a heading. |
| Robot.com | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f85060f2-e490-4793-b11f-86d41a6a2cd0) | Cells are deliberately unequal: one tall cell holds only a headline and is otherwise empty, three hold a single figure each, and one is inverted to dark and holds a pure graphic — so the grid has a loud cell, quiet cells, and one silent cell. |
| Spade | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/34a2151f-c9b2-4121-b790-8e85202fe9ca) | Cells are marked by four corner crop marks instead of a border or a rounded rectangle, with a tiny kicker pinned top-left and the value anchored to the bottom edge — the cell boundary is implied, so the number reads as printed on the page. |
| Gumroad | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/679cf598-2beb-4257-8152-d2efddac64f7) | An enormous figure is the cell's whole content and illustrated objects cross its baseline and overlap its digits, then continue past the band into the section below — the escape is what stitches two sections together. |
| Cosmos | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8f4d0cbd-ca46-40a5-9014-2d42195250ac) | Mixed media sits in a strict column rhythm at native aspect ratios with zero card chrome, so cell heights vary with the content and the only thing holding the grid is the gutter. |
| Nite Riot | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/75986401-f96a-4ade-b171-fb632386a8a9) | Cells are rotated a few degrees off-axis and allowed to overlap each other and run off all four edges, turning the grid into a physical pile — proof a grid can be implied by a rejected baseline rather than obeyed. |
| Epidemic Sound | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/600ccc40-fda1-41ae-b6f7-408369b9113f) | A full-bleed photo mosaic where the only cell boundary is the hairline gap between images and no cell carries a headline — the grid is read as one image, so it can act as a palate cleanser between two dense sections. |
| Bento | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/268f752c-e6af-4349-848f-10f6bf83ed40) | Photographs are given white borders and tilted at varying small angles in a single row that runs off both viewport edges, so the row reads as prints laid on a desk and the cut-off at each edge implies more. |
| Dropbox | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1bc6f80b-c5fa-40ce-aad6-19649241a75a) | The main media cell is centred and small portrait tiles are placed *outside* it in the left and right margins at staggered heights — satellites live in the gutter, which makes the centre cell feel like it has depth behind it. |
| Runway | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/079fc906-e760-487a-8636-53aba021685c) | A strict three-column grid where the image is a flush rectangle and the kicker plus headline sit *below and outside* it rather than inside a card — removing the container is what stops it reading as a dashboard. |
| Claude | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/229496fd-8db0-4230-b10b-573b5b028300) | Cells are unequal widths on a two-column rhythm and the label and title are anchored to the bottom of each cell while the illustration floats in the space above — bottom-anchoring the text makes uneven cell heights survivable. |
| IFTTT | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/49ec32ab-8219-4f42-ba0c-788a8e34d0cc) | A row of four equal narrow cells sits above a row of two wide cells of different weights, and one wide cell lets a screenshot bleed hard to its own right edge — the change of row rhythm is the section's only hierarchy device. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenPhone | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/368e9368-e7ad-44c1-88fb-ed73b0d3cd04) | Six identical rounded-rect cards, each icon → headline → two lines of body | This is the exact bento the brief bans. Every cell is the same weight, so nothing is a lead and the section carries no argument — it's a list wearing a grid costume. |
| Airtable | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3f3fc633-f303-4b1b-97e5-a6661f617a43) | Sixteen uniform icon cards in a 4×4 block | At that count the grid becomes an index, not a story. A marketing chapter has to rank its claims; a 4×4 of equals refuses to. |
| Retool | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0daad8f7-cdde-4ef2-a454-2a26d2bbe0a5) | Gradient-filled feature cells containing product-UI screenshots | Gradient fills plus fake-dashboard cell contents — two hard-avoids — and the screenshots are too small to read, so they function as texture pretending to be evidence. |
| Deel | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b3a3012d-21ca-4800-8591-1c13d4743935) | Statistic rows with a small decorative gradient icon sitting beside each number | The icon competes with the numeral for the same eye stop and adds no information; if the numeral is the cell, nothing else may sit on its baseline. |
| Garden | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/dec86b40-809c-4986-9f52-d9e5157be7c4) | Five equal columns of pale outline-illustration cards | All five cells are identical in weight and the illustrations are near-invisible, so the section has no entry point and the eye simply slides past it. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "asymmetric feature grid where cells are different sizes and each cell is numbered with a large numeral"
- `search_sections`: "section with huge numerals as the dominant graphic element marking steps or statistics in an editorial layout"
- `search_sections`: "bento grid mixing photography video and text cells of unequal size where an element overflows past its cell edge"

**MCP note:** the first query returned almost entirely the icon-card bento we are avoiding — useful as a refusal set, useless as adopted material. The numeral query had to be re-worded away from "numbered cell" toward "huge numerals as the dominant graphic" before it surfaced anything with real scale. Genuine cell-boundary *escape* is thinly indexed; Gumroad and Nite Riot are the only two clean examples returned across all three queries.
