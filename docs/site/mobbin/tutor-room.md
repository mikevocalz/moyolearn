# Mobbin pass: tutor-room

Chapter 5 of the Moyo marketing site. Target moves: a product or person introduced over a flooded colour field; whiteboard / collaboration surfaces; progress rings and paths; product-truth cards.

**Structure only.** Several of the whiteboard references below are wall-to-wall product screenshots — the takeaway is always *how the surface is framed and cropped*, never their chrome.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mural | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0305b0cb-2552-4f0b-8ad8-f666166e4173) | The flooded colour band ends partway down and the collaboration surface is dropped so it straddles the boundary — the artefact breaks the colour field's bottom edge, which is what gives the flood depth instead of making it a coloured box. |
| Wise | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f9808f26-b226-4511-b935-21e683e0b408) | The flooded band contains exactly one enormous line of type and one small pill button and nothing else, then the page returns to neutral for the list beneath — the flood is used as a full-width punctuation mark between two content zones. |
| Oryzo | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9d67f8ac-8069-4a4b-999a-8ae18e7debf0) | The object is lit and floated in the centre of a single dark field with copy split to both margins, so a plain product shot becomes a room the object is standing in. |
| Analogue Agency | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8dae6616-3341-438e-ac6e-cff4ce230cc4) | The section is split hard down the middle — a near-empty pale half carrying a short label and paragraph, and a full-bleed portrait half — and the copy is vertically centred against a person's face, so the introduction is a diptych rather than an overlay. |
| Daylight | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b9729ea8-5d0f-4cd1-bd67-77d6f9d835e0) | A full-bleed photograph of the device *in a real hand* carries the headline bottom-left and three tiny claim columns bottom-right along the same baseline — product truth is docked to the bottom edge of the evidence, not floated over its middle. |
| Oura Ring | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/70f3a470-35a9-48cf-ada4-59a44df8cb5f) | A single progress arc and two small metric cards are anchored *into* a real photograph at the depth of the scene, with a thin time-scale rule down the left edge — the ring is treated as an object in the room, not a widget on a card. |
| Sketch | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c6fdb3c9-c8dc-4758-a1ac-fbbfae854618) | The actual chapter-by-chapter lesson list with real durations occupies two-thirds of the section and a sidebar card carries the mentor, the totals and the single CTA — the syllabus itself is the proof, and the card only summarises what is already visible. |
| Miro | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c759aef1-12cc-4e2e-a1a5-8914ef8732e3) | The path is a vertical spine with a filled dot at the current position and a hollow connector between steps, and each step's card can expand in place — progress is shown as a route with a "you are here", not a percentage. |
| Sprout Social | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/90206d9e-4335-4c49-a499-e8f664838cf9) | The progress line sits directly between the completion sentence and the Resume button, so the reader gets state, then evidence of that state, then the one action — and a 0% state is shown without apology or nagging. |
| Miro | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/42318d55-aa9d-4ec6-9097-69075da82302) | Product UI is shown as small tiles *inside* photographs of people actually using it, cropped to a 2×2 with one tall cell — mixing the tool and the room in one grid stops the section reading as a screenshot dump. |
| Miro | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/071f7650-b917-400d-9fe1-458bd9b7bb5b) | The canvas is cropped so it bleeds off both sides inside a flooded band, then three equal claim columns sit underneath separated by short top rules — surface first at full width, then the three things it means. |
| Uxcel | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/68e66915-517e-4cfa-a65b-515db70c8f82) | Two unequal cells each show a *real* measurement artefact — a skill radar with a member list beside it — so the "we measure progress" claim is carried by the actual chart rather than by an icon standing in for one. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Figma | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/087ddedc-e223-4bf8-b82e-7fca47ceee5a) | Whiteboard floating on a large purple organic blob background | Blob decoration plus a purple field — two hard-avoids — and the blob adds a second, competing silhouette behind an already busy surface. |
| Uxcel | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/08777df1-96cb-44d9-8c6e-ced59134d112) | "Gamified, habit-forming experience" cell with trophies, a flame streak icon and a leaderboard of named users | Engagement-pressure mechanics with a leaderboard of children is a non-starter under Moyo's children's-surfaces rules, and "habit-forming" is not a claim we can make about minors. |
| Canva | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f10c2563-bdcb-4ac6-89b7-086a51491b36) | Course banner with a star rating, a student count and an empty progress bar over a teal gradient | The progress bar is decorative (it is at zero for everyone) and the star rating imports an app-store register into a teaching surface; a progress indicator that never means anything is worse than none. |
| Figma | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2a2c9e57-fcc8-4d15-a593-c18509dd8054) | Overlapping product panels with duplicate "Get started" buttons appearing twice within one viewport | Two identical primary CTAs in one section means neither is primary, and the stacked panels crop each other into unreadable slivers. |
| Skillshare | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/441ada2d-0e6c-4b24-838e-bd25b5ba92e7) | Alternating screenshot-left / paragraph-right rows with no crop and no scale change | The screenshots are shown whole and small, so nothing is legible and every row weighs the same — a product tour with no argument and no focal point. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "product or person introduced on a single flooded saturated colour field taking the whole section"
- `search_sections`: "progress ring or learning path visualisation showing completed lessons and streaks"
- `search_sections`: "shared whiteboard or canvas collaboration surface shown with multiple cursors and sticky notes"

**MCP note:** the progress query returned mostly LMS course-catalogue chrome — the exact "corporate LMS styling" the brief bans — so the adopted rows here are the few that show a *route* or a real measurement rather than a completion bar. True progress **rings** barely appear outside wearables; Oura is the only clean one returned, and it is worth noting the best example came from a hardware brand rather than an education one.
