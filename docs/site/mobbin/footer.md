# Mobbin pass: footer

Chapter 9 of the Moyo marketing site. Target moves: playful "desk at rest" footers; sitemap organisation; compliance lines; hover easter eggs that fire **once** rather than looping.

**Structure only.** The footer is where a site either confirms it was made by people who care or admits it stopped caring at 80% scroll — take the organisation logic and the one moment of play, not the illustration style.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opennote | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3dbc7c17-d1c9-4490-a394-33c61e52f471) | Faint hand-drawn margin doodles — a frog, an equation, "REMEMBER FOR LATER" — are scattered *below* the link columns at the very bottom of the page, so the desk-at-rest moment lives underneath the functional footer instead of competing with it. The single closest reference in this whole pass to Moyo's Tactile Learning register. |
| Robot.com | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5cc26aaf-f102-42e9-984a-3c8768918494) | Beneath the compliance line sits a full-width dot-matrix panel rendering two eyes — a character that is *only* implied, made from the page's own grid, and reading as the product idling rather than as a mascot. |
| Fourmula AI | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d54eba0f-6c02-4e36-80b9-4f47320d66ce) | The same idea aimed at a single glyph: a dot grid resolves into a cursor arrow at the bottom edge — one shape, one read, nothing animated, so it rewards noticing rather than demanding attention. |
| Swap | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0cc6183c-2921-48ea-bfe9-d7c3d60bfed4) | The email field is drawn as a banner being towed by a small illustrated plane, and a continuous line-drawn village runs along the very bottom edge as a single horizontal scene — the play is a *strip* along the base, which keeps it from interrupting the six link columns above. |
| Vucko | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/325d9889-5eae-493d-926a-a959e8e99005) | A short two-column sitemap sits at the top under a hairline rule, an enormous wordmark occupies the lower half, and "Back to top ↑" and the copyright share the wordmark's baseline — the mark is the footer's mass and the utility links are the small print. |
| Mixpanel | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1d050a7d-840b-4110-bb1b-303ae9fd76e8) | A "Prefers reduced motion" toggle sits in the footer's bottom-left beside the wordmark, and a "We're hiring" chip is nested inline in the Company column — accessibility is an available control in the footer, not a claim on a policy page. |
| Mural | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6406438a-171a-4d5f-bf51-71c4e03d1549) | Six labelled columns above, then a distinct lower band holding the wordmark, the privacy statement, a "Your Privacy Choices" control with its own opt-out icon, and the corporate entity disclosure — the legal band is visually separated from the sitemap so the compliance text does not dilute the navigation. |
| Complex Law | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d33c835f-2d6e-4ce1-b827-23703ef3a607) | The left column carries a plain-language description of the firm, the registered office address, the regulator, and the regulatory marks, with registration and VAT numbers on the final line — the full "who is legally responsible for this" statement, which a site serving children should treat as mandatory. |
| In Common With | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f1844604-7696-4970-b30e-43a7669e9ee1) | The top band is *contact* — email, phone, two physical addresses with opening hours — and the link columns sit beneath it; leading the footer with a way to reach a human, before the sitemap, is a trust move that costs nothing. |
| Lightship | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5271842f-2780-414a-b9f6-aed741bbb222) | The footer opens with a short paragraph saying what the company is, then the newsletter field, and on the right a product list where each row shows its availability state — the footer restates the offer to a reader who arrived deep and scrolled past everything. |
| Employment Hero | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6efab40e-de5e-4dff-bf1c-b28c818a934d) | The subscribe block uses **two separate unchecked consent boxes** — one for data processing, one for marketing — and a region selector sits on the compliance line; consent is split by purpose and nothing is pre-ticked. |
| Spade | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6d088b40-a574-4e35-905c-bff022cfc5d5) | Three tight link columns on the left and the newsletter isolated on the right with the input marked by corner crop brackets, and a single compliance line right-aligned at the bottom — a footer that stays one screen-band tall and still holds everything. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Escape Cafe | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/55b1204a-dc3e-487f-9c8c-7de3229edf6c) | A persistent "Get 20% OFF" tab pinned to the left edge and a rotating announcement rail on the right, both overlaying the footer | Two persistent overlays clipping the footer content, and a discount tab that follows the reader everywhere. This is the looping-attention-grab the brief rules out, and on a children's product a permanent discount tab is indefensible. |
| Dovetail | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/bc5302dd-40da-4974-9ca8-8ee93fccf2fb) | Promotional article card with a purple 3D render embedded as a fifth footer column | The footer becomes another content-marketing placement; a bright render in the footer's corner pulls the eye away from the compliance links, which is exactly backwards. |
| Employment Hero | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6efab40e-de5e-4dff-bf1c-b28c818a934d) | Five stacked link columns plus a region list plus a social column, ~30 links with no visual grouping beyond their headings | Adopted the consent structure, refused the volume: past roughly four columns a footer sitemap stops being navigable and becomes a wall, and Moyo's audience split (family / school / learner) needs far fewer, clearer doors. |
| Mural | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6406438a-171a-4d5f-bf51-71c4e03d1549) | Six columns totalling around forty links directly above the legal band | Same failure at larger scale — adopted the separated legal band and refused the sitemap above it. |
| Robot.com | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5cc26aaf-f102-42e9-984a-3c8768918494) | Legal and social links set in a very low-contrast grey against the dark footer field | Adopted the dot-matrix easter egg, refused the contrast: privacy policy and terms are the two links most likely to be needed under stress, and they are the faintest text on the page here. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "playful oversized website footer with a big wordmark, sitemap columns and a hidden surprise"
- `search_sections`: "footer with newsletter signup, legal compliance links and company registration details at the bottom of the page"

**MCP note:** hover easter eggs are by definition invisible to a static index, so nothing here can confirm the "fires once rather than looping" requirement — the three closest references (Opennote, Robot.com, Fourmula) are all *resting-state* eggs that reward noticing without motion, which is arguably the safer answer anyway. Worth recording that the strongest desk-at-rest footer in the entire pass, Opennote, comes from a study-notes product — the nearest neighbour to Moyo's own category.
