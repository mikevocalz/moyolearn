# Mobbin pass: pricing

Chapter 8 of the Moyo marketing site. Target moves: a single honest plan card (not a comparison table); strikethrough / early-bird treatments; trial lines; what-you-get lists that stay short.

Per the `mobbin-pass` skill this is a paywall-class surface, so it got **two** queries and the refusal list is held to the dark-pattern checklist (countdown timers, hidden "no", pre-checked upsells, guilt loops, six-option first decisions). Moyo's own rule stands above all of it: **no price, paywall or upgrade prompt may ever render on a learner surface** — everything here belongs to the adult route only.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ClassPass | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/196d075d-daff-4ea9-bdf3-711046c02876) | A horizontal trial timeline runs from "free trial" to "first billed month" with the reminder email marked as a labelled node *between* them, and the plans only appear below it — the price is read after the reassurance, and the reminder is drawn as a promise rather than written as a disclaimer. |
| Superpower | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/09904386-61ba-4210-a9f6-a025335da025) | One wide card: claim headline, price, and the annual equivalent stacked on the left; the five-item inclusion list on the right; a hairline rule; then a secondary "Read FAQs" and a primary action side by side, with the awkward regional surcharge stated in a footnote directly beneath them rather than buried. |
| Reflect | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e42c8789-8709-4d25-8778-8f18b6cb3719) | The headline states the pricing model in plain words before the number appears, the billing condition is set small and immediately to the right of the figure, six short items sit in two columns, and the trial button comes last — model, price, contents, action, in that order. |
| Skillshare | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1402b3eb-5585-4634-8884-807faa1bd563) | One outlined card holding name, who it is for, the price on its own line, the CTA, and only then the checked list — putting the action *above* the list means the list reads as confirmation rather than as a hurdle. |
| Intercom | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8ef342a4-9bd4-48f1-ae25-ae71812ceee8) | A small card with a dotted rule under the label and the numeral set several sizes larger than the "From" and "per month" that flank it on the same baseline — the qualifiers stay attached to the number instead of becoming a separate line of small print. |
| Intercom | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/859354c1-833d-4985-bfbe-44a77a942639) | The price is written as a two-line formula and the trial length is set in the *smallest* type directly beneath it, above a secondary "View pricing" and a primary "Start free trial" — the honest complexity is shown rather than smoothed into one fake number. |
| Greptile | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3acb1f30-cc02-4b04-9dbe-b6425a2b55ed) | Two eligibility-based offers sit as plain equal cells, each with a bracketed kicker, one sentence of criteria, and an "Apply for…" button — a discount framed as an application you qualify for, with no clock and no scarcity. |
| Craft | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1d233811-a8c1-46dc-9b29-eb4c12feba62) | The struck original price sits immediately left of the new price on the same baseline at the same size class, with the monthly equivalent below in small type — the comparison happens in one eye stop instead of across two lines. |
| Headspace | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ce332309-3c44-4814-80c7-0f8173e1eda6) | Each option is a selectable row where the *trial length* is the bold line and the price is the quiet second line, and the billing consequence after the trial is stated as a footnote sitting directly above the button — the sentence you need is the last thing you read before you commit. |
| Squarespace | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e19368ea-a33a-4b0a-8490-79c227a9c5ad) | "No credit card required. Cancel anytime." is a single small line placed between the headline and the button, with no card, no icon and no box — the two objections that actually stop people are answered in one line at the exact point of decision. |
| Deezer | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a21da601-33af-4630-902c-e8c17cfa6542) | A bordered notice strip stating when the trial reminder will arrive is placed *above* the plan block, so the exit is described before the entrance is offered. |
| Parker AI | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/240ebeb3-dfb9-4fe3-8bc0-9f069adab348) | "What's Included" is lifted out into its own full-height card beside the tiers, with the tiers reduced to a name, a number and one qualifying sentence each — stating shared inclusions once removes the repeated checklists that make comparison tables unreadable. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Craft | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1d233811-a8c1-46dc-9b29-eb4c12feba62) | "Black Friday weekend — 50% off for life" banner with a code and a "Limited Time Only. Valid until…" line, repeated as a sticky top bar | Adopted the strikethrough geometry, refused the pressure around it. Manufactured deadlines are on the ban list, and a family deciding about a child's education must never be rushed by a clock. |
| Superlist | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/da254d5f-aa46-4097-8231-a40547da5472) | Four tiers plus a monthly/yearly toggle plus crown and sparkle tier icons, all above the fold | A six-variable first decision. Every extra axis raises the chance the reader picks nothing, and the crown iconography sorts customers into ranks — the wrong emotional register for a learning product. |
| Cursor | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/350dda44-322b-4e9c-ace3-1ce3adbf894a) | Four columns whose lists all read "Everything in the previous plan, plus…" | The chained lists mean no single column is comprehensible on its own, so understanding the product requires reading the whole matrix left to right — comparison work offloaded onto the reader. |
| Headspace | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c1d76f10-aed2-4b46-acfb-0e1690a5f615) | The costlier annual option pre-selected and flagged "Best value", with the cheaper option unchecked below it | A pre-checked upsell. The default should be the smallest commitment; making the larger one the path of least resistance is a decision made *for* the buyer. |
| Deezer | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a21da601-33af-4630-902c-e8c17cfa6542) | Student discount presented as a separate collapsed row below the main plan | Adopted the reminder strip, refused this: if a group qualifies for a lower price, that must be visible at the same level as the headline price, not folded away where only a persistent reader finds it. |
| ClassPass | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/196d075d-daff-4ea9-bdf3-711046c02876) | Auto-enrolment terms and late-cancel fees set in pale grey small type at the very top, above the fold's real content | Adopted the timeline, refused the typographic hiding. The one sentence that describes what will be charged is the one sentence that must not be the faintest text on the page. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "pricing section with a single plan card and a short list of what is included, no comparison table"
- `search_sections`: "free trial offer showing a strikethrough original price and an early bird or founding member discount with the trial terms stated" *(second, paywall-mandated pass)*

**MCP note:** the single-card query still returned mostly multi-tier grids — a genuinely single-plan pricing page is rare in the index, and Reflect and Superpower are the only two clean examples. **Early-bird / founding-member pricing produced nothing honest**: every discount returned was either a manufactured holiday deadline (Craft) or an eligibility programme (Greptile). If Moyo runs an early-bird, Greptile's "apply because you qualify" framing is the only referenced pattern here that survives the ban list.
