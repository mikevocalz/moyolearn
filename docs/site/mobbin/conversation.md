# Mobbin pass: conversation

Chapter 3 of the Moyo marketing site. Target moves: showing a teaching/guidance exchange as **artifacts rather than chat bubbles** — annotated documents, before/after correction, step-by-step reveals. The chat-bubble mock is precisely what this chapter has to replace, so the refusal list matters as much as the adopted list here.

**Structure only.** Read every row as "where the correction sits relative to the work", never as a style note.

## Adopted

| App | Link | Structural move adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammarly | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1b0448c5-83f8-4044-a403-e54f30ce316c) | The learner's sentence is its own small card and the explanation is a *second* card overlapping its lower-right corner — the guidance is physically attached to the work rather than living in a separate conversation column. |
| Grammarly | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e8c582a5-165d-4b90-811a-76dd59488207) | The original sentence stays visible with strike-through on the changed words while the rewritten sentence sits in an attached panel beneath it, so the correction is legible as a *diff* and the learner's own words are never deleted from view. |
| Grammarly | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1c087d5a-d8d8-4a69-b800-f022c027e3bc) | Two artifact stacks are placed diagonally across the section, each paired with its own headline on the opposite side — alternating the artifact/text sides down the page keeps a long explanation from turning into a feature list. |
| Ploy | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9138eef2-4217-4b65-bdac-b9f7909c6d40) | Before and after are one table of paired rows sharing a horizontal rule, with an arrow glyph in the header as the only transition marker — the reader can scan a single row and get one complete "was / now" without leaving the line. |
| OpenAI | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/88a31de0-8431-4a5e-a8df-8761600e6676) | Two results are shown flush side by side with the *same* prompt printed as a caption under both — repeating the input verbatim under each artifact is what makes the comparison honest instead of promotional. |
| Superpower | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f95688f7-2ed1-4663-8d6d-2fb686648996) | Two options are represented as photographed stacks of physical paper, each with its own "view sample" download control, and the shared CTA sits centred *below* both — you compare artifacts, then commit once. |
| Descript | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/02baf104-3af7-400c-8cb5-19660df2763c) | Two outlined panels carry a small pill label straddling their top edge ("Without" / "With"), so the label reads as a tab clipped onto the panel rather than a heading floating above it. |
| Samara | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/001f0854-a5f2-42e5-b54f-349fdf95799a) | Steps hang off a single vertical spine with dots as anchors, and the text alternates left and right of the spine while photographs bleed off the outer edges — the timeline is the structure, and each step gets its own real artifact. |
| Ditto | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/74f2a8a6-5b4f-4590-8ae1-7a9ae2231aa5) | A stage list runs down the left rail as sticky context while the artifact panel on the right swaps per stage — the reader always knows which step they are on because the index never leaves. |
| Firecrawl | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/363363b9-a122-4092-8c41-962f962e6a46) | Three steps sit in a narrow left column as selectable rows with a thick marker on the active one, and the right pane shows the real output for that step — the exchange is shown as *input list → produced artifact*, never as a dialogue. |
| FLORA | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/35657c50-f894-4f9e-9a02-7ec46c414587) | Each step's numeral and verb sit at the top of a tall cell with the artifact partially cropped beneath it and the cells overlapping in a horizontal run — the crop implies the step continues past the frame instead of being a finished tile. |
| Wild | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/eef9343d-0d21-41e4-9524-54462b7612db) | One continuous data graphic spans the full width and four step captions are placed underneath at the x-positions they describe — the steps annotate a single object rather than each getting a card of their own. |

## Refused

| App | Link | Pattern | Why refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Framer | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c809d4ab-d6fb-446e-a6c6-d57e72e5fbda) | Two floating chat bubbles with circular avatars inside a gradient card | The exact thing this chapter exists to replace. The bubbles show a *vibe of collaboration* and zero actual work; nothing on screen is the thing being taught. |
| Dropbox | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d8e03e13-2b29-4daf-a716-2f1c6084b2e1) | Phone mock with a comment thread and a "type a comment" input under a file preview | The artifact is reduced to a thumbnail and the thread dominates. For Moyo the ratio must invert — the student's work is the subject and the guidance is the annotation on it. |
| Cake Equity | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3d116059-6b9d-418e-aa5d-6938c52efe82) | "Old school vs new school" panels with sad/smiley emoji headers, sparkles and a gradient | Emoji as the comparison's argument, plus sparkle decoration — a mocking before-state is the wrong register entirely for a child's earlier attempt. |
| V7 | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/17e4b22d-7c8d-45ff-a87c-47b61b089da6) | Before panel rendered dead-black and grey, after panel rendered in a glowing saturated field | Shaming the "before" with darkness. In a tutoring context the previous draft belongs to the learner, so it must be shown neutrally, never as the villain of the section. |
| Titan | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0d103afa-c85e-4568-a98a-0a1e7cbd5e8f) | "How it works" as three collapsed accordion rows | The steps are hidden behind chevrons and no step shows an output, so the process is asserted rather than demonstrated — the opposite of an artifact-led reveal. |
| Duolingo | [section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3d2e6dfe-6b52-47b8-ae46-cd350198e083) | Body copy paired with a generic magnifying-glass-and-beaker spot illustration | A stock "rigour" illustration next to a paragraph about standards; the graphic carries no information and would read as clip art beside real student work. |

## Queries used

- `search_sections`: "section showing marked-up annotated document with corrections and margin notes on a page of student work"
- `search_sections`: "before and after side by side comparison showing a piece of work improved, with the original on one side and the revised version on the other"
- `search_sections`: "how it works section revealing a process one step at a time as the user scrolls, with each step showing the artifact produced"

**MCP note:** the index has very little *handwritten* annotation — pencil marks, margin notes on real paper simply are not represented. Everything returned for the first query was software-UI annotation (Grammarly, Figma, Dovetail), so the paper-annotation half of this chapter has no Mobbin precedent and will have to be designed from the Tactile Learning half of the design language rather than referenced.
