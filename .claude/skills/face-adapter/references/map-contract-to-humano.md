## The nine-vs-52 gap

`LipShape` (`packages/avatar/src/presence/humano.ts:184`) declares nine morphs. `lipFromOpenness` (`:218`) writes those nine from one openness scalar. That is nine of 52.

The gap is not a rig limitation. It is a limitation of the input:

| Input | Path | Reach |
|---|---|---|
| `HumanoInput.mouth` (scalar) | `lipFromOpenness` → `setMorph` per `LipShape` key | 9 morphs |
| `HumanoInput.face` (named `Shape`) | `setMorph` per key present (`:739`) | all 52 |

Bind the contract to `face`. The wiring already exists — `tutor-avatar-3d.native.tsx:491` passes both, and `face` takes precedence when non-null (`packages/avatar/src/presence/humano.ts:738`).

Note the docstring drift while you are there: the comment above `LipShape` says "twelve mouth morphs" and the interface declares nine. Fix one of them.

## Replacing the merge

Today, per mesh, per frame (`packages/avatar/src/presence/humano.ts:735-755`):

```
morphTargetInfluences.fill(0)
face ? setMorph(each face entry) : setMorph(each lip entry)
input.emotion  -> maxMorph(each entry)        // per-channel max
maxMorph('eyeBlinkLeft',  frame.eyeBlinkLeft)
maxMorph('eyeBlinkRight', frame.eyeBlinkRight)
maxMorph('eyeWideLeft',   frame.eyesWide)
maxMorph('eyeWideRight',  frame.eyesWide)
setMorph(each gaze entry)
maxMorph('browInnerUp', 0.2 * beatEnv)
```

Four `maxMorph` sites, plus the emotion loop, is the universal per-channel `max()` the audit names. Replace with a contract-resolved composite:

1. Clear to zero — keep this. The morphs are absolute weights and a stale one never decays on its own.
2. Speech writes its `exclusive` VIS channels.
3. Expression writes its `exclusive` AU channels, and its `capped-additive` contributions clamped to `expressionMax` and summed under speech.
4. Blink writes `eyeBlink*` exclusively.
5. Gaze writes the eight `eyeLook*` exclusively — `setMorph`, which it already is.
6. The beat's `browInnerUp` accent is an expression-layer contribution, so it goes through the AU1 channel's resolution rather than its own `maxMorph`. Prosody and gesture moving together is right; a fifth independent writer is not.

Assert one writer per `exclusive` channel per frame. `claimFaceWriter` (`packages/avatar/src/store.ts`) already enforces this for the whole expression vector; the contract does it per channel.

## Bones the face adapter touches

Head and neck pose belongs to the body layer, not the face. If a face adapter moves it anyway, three constraints from the `humano.ts` header apply and none is optional:

- The skin binds to `DEF-*` bones only. `HUMANO_BONES` (`:76`) names them: `DEF-spine` through `DEF-spine.006` for the chain, `DEF-eye.L/R` as the gaze anchor.
- The control bones `head`, `neck` and `chest` are **not** ancestors of any DEF bone in this export. Writing them moves nothing — this is the measured failure the header records.
- Every torso/head rotation must be mirrored, as the same world rotation, onto the twin chain in `TWINS` (`:131`). The eyeballs, teeth and arms hang off that chain; rotating the head skin alone leaves the eyes where the head used to be.

`TWINS` and `STANCE` are exported from `presence/humano.ts` but **not** re-exported through `@acme/avatar/body` (`packages/avatar/src/body-index.ts:32-45`). Import from the module path or add them to the barrel; do not copy the values.

## Verify the binding

- Every contract channel resolves to a morph target present in the manifest. Fail the build on a miss.
- `skeletonHash` matches the one the binding was built against. A changed hash means re-run `avatar-rig-audit` and re-verify.
- `scripts/impossible-face.mjs` is clean over a golden performance rendered through the binding.
- Lip articulation stays legible at every tone's peak intensity — `celebrate-big` is the highest smile in the palette, so test there.
